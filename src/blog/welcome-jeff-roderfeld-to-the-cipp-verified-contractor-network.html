CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome Jeff Roderfeld, LLC of St. Charles, Missouri, as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for the state of Missouri.

Missouri has been an open territory on our map, and the St. Louis metro area's mix of older sewer laterals and onsite wastewater systems in the surrounding counties made it a priority to fill. We are glad to add a contractor whose registration covers both sides of that work.

About Jeff Roderfeld, LLC

Jeff Roderfeld, LLC is based in St. Charles, Missouri, and is represented on the CIPP Verified Contractor Network by Scott Miller. The company works on sewer lines and onsite wastewater systems across St. Charles, St. Louis, Jefferson, Lincoln, Warren, and Montgomery counties.

The company is registered with the Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services as an Advanced-level installer for onsite wastewater treatment systems, in addition to its sewer line work. That dual registration gives it a broader view of a property's whole wastewater picture than a contractor who only handles the line inside the house.

Sewer Line Service

Jeff Roderfeld, LLC diagnoses and repairs sewer line problems across the St. Louis metro counties, giving property owners an alternative to a full excavation when the pipe's condition allows it.

For properties outside municipal sewer service, the company's Advanced-level state registration covers septic and onsite wastewater treatment system installation and repair as well.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business and customer record, and the credentials the business brings to sewer and wastewater work. Jeff Roderfeld, LLC's state registration and multi-county service area cleared those checks.
