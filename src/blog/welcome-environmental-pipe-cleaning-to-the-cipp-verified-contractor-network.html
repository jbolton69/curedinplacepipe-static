CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome Environmental Pipe Cleaning, Inc. of Ashland, Virginia, as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for Virginia and North Carolina.

Not every pipe problem is a homeowner's sewer lateral. Hospitals, schools, shopping centers, and municipal mains all run on pipe that eventually needs to be rehabilitated, and the job looks different at that scale: bigger diameters, more connections, and a building or a street that cannot simply be shut down for an excavation. Environmental Pipe Cleaning built its business around exactly that kind of work, and we are glad to have a dedicated commercial and municipal specialist representing the network in Virginia and North Carolina.

About Environmental Pipe Cleaning

Environmental Pipe Cleaning, Inc. is led by Dave Hawes out of Ashland, Virginia. The company made a deliberate decision some time ago to stop taking on residential pipe lining jobs and focus entirely on commercial, industrial, and government or municipal clients. That focus shows up in the equipment and crew training: the company is set up for larger-diameter mains and multi-connection systems, not single-family laterals.

Before any liner goes into a pipe, a commercial or municipal line generally needs more cleaning and more documentation than a residential job. Environmental Pipe Cleaning handles that front-end work in-house: root and scale removal, high-pressure hydro jetting, and a full video inspection, so the facility manager, general contractor, or municipal engineer on the other end has a clear picture of the pipe's condition before committing to a repair method.

Trenchless Services

Sewer and Drain Cleaning

Industrial and municipal lines accumulate scale, grease, and debris that an ordinary jetting truck is not built for. Environmental Pipe Cleaning's cleaning equipment is sized for commercial mains and prepares the host pipe properly before any lining work begins.

Video Camera Inspection

Every project starts with a camera run through the line, giving the client documented evidence of the pipe's condition and a basis for deciding between a lining, a point repair, or a full replacement.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

For commercial, industrial, and municipal mains that still hold their shape, Environmental Pipe Cleaning installs a cured-in-place liner that becomes a new, jointless pipe inside the old one, avoiding an excavation through a parking lot, a street, or a building's footprint.

Service Area

Environmental Pipe Cleaning is headquartered in Ashland, Virginia, and covers the entire state along with North Carolina, working on commercial, industrial, and government or municipal projects only.

Property managers, general contractors, and municipal engineers in Virginia and North Carolina can learn more about Environmental Pipe Cleaning's services at environmentalpipe.com, the company's website, or on the Environmental Pipe Cleaning profile here on CuredInPlacePipe.net.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business, and the equipment and track record the crews bring to cured-in-place and related trenchless methods. Environmental Pipe Cleaning's narrow, deliberate focus on commercial and municipal work is exactly the kind of specialization this program was built to recognize.
