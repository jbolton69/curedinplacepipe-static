CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome Hill Services, Inc. of Memphis, Tennessee, as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for Tennessee and Arkansas.

Tennessee and Arkansas have been open territory on our map, and few contractors anywhere bring the kind of institutional history Hill Services does to that gap. We are glad to add a century-old Mid-South company to represent both states.

About Hill Services

Hill Services, Inc. has served the Mid-South since 1923, and the company is represented on the CIPP Verified Contractor Network by Nick Crone. From its headquarters on Covington Way in Memphis, plus branch operations in Arkansas, the company has grown into one of the larger commercial and industrial service providers in the region.

Hill Services' trenchless lineup includes pipe relining, manhole rehabilitation, mainline camera inspection, and point repair, alongside its broader plumbing and HVAC work. That combination lets the company diagnose a pipe problem on camera and fix it from the inside, without excavating a street or a parking lot, on jobs ranging from a single home's sewer lateral up to commercial and industrial mainlines.

Trenchless Services

Mainline Camera Inspection

Hill Services documents the condition of a line on camera before recommending a repair, whether the job is a single-family home or a commercial mainline.

Pipe Relining

The company's cured-in-place relining restores a damaged sewer or drain line from the inside, without opening the ground above it.

Manhole Rehabilitation

For municipal and commercial systems, Hill Services rehabilitates manholes in addition to the pipe runs between them, a service that most residential-focused contractors do not offer.

Point Repair

When only a short section of pipe has failed, Hill Services can repair that section alone rather than relining or replacing the whole run.

Service Area

Hill Services is headquartered in Memphis and serves Tennessee and Arkansas, with branch operations supporting both states.

Property owners in Tennessee and Arkansas can learn more about Hill Services at hillishere.com, or on the Hill Services profile here on CuredInPlacePipe.net.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business and customer record, and the equipment and services the crews bring to trenchless and related work. A century in business and a full trenchless service lineup cleared those checks easily.
