CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome SLB Pipe Solutions, Inc. of Bedford, Indiana, as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for the state of Indiana.

Indiana has been an open territory on our map, and south-central Indiana's older housing stock, cast iron drain lines, and clay sewer laterals are exactly the kind of pipe that makes a strong case for lining instead of digging. We are glad to add a contractor who can give Bedford-area property owners that option.

About SLB Pipe Solutions

SLB Pipe Solutions, Inc. is owned by Eric Spreen and based in Bedford, Indiana. The company lines failing sewer and drain pipes from the inside, which means a damaged line can be restored without digging up the yard, driveway, or parking lot above it.

Every job starts the same way: a camera inspection so the customer can see the actual condition of the pipe before any work is scheduled. SLB keeps the cleaning, lining, and follow-up inspection in-house rather than subcontracting any step, so the same crew that diagnoses the problem is the crew that fixes it and confirms the result.

Trenchless Services

Sewer Camera Inspection

A video inspection locates cracks, root intrusion, and offset joints before any repair method is chosen, and it gives the customer a clear look at what is actually wrong with the line.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

SLB's core service restores a damaged sewer or drain line by curing a resin-saturated liner inside the existing pipe, creating a smooth, jointless pipe within the old one without opening the ground above it.

Follow-Up Inspection

After a liner cures, the camera goes back through the line so the customer can see the finished repair end to end.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business and customer record, and the equipment the crews bring to cured-in-place work. SLB Pipe Solutions cleared those checks as an owner-operated, lining-focused contractor serving its home region.
