CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome Plumber of Tucson as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for the state of Arizona.

Arizona has been an open territory on our map, and Tucson's desert soil brings its own version of the problem every trenchless program exists to solve: aggressive mesquite and palo verde roots that find their way into older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. We are glad to add a long-standing local contractor who can offer Tucson-area property owners a no-dig option.

About Plumber of Tucson

Plumber of Tucson has served homeowners and businesses across the Tucson area since 2005, under owner Christopher Reid. The company handles the full range of residential plumbing work, from routine repairs to emergency calls, and trenchless sewer line repair is part of that lineup for customers who want a damaged sewer line fixed without a trench across the yard.

The company is licensed and insured in Arizona and backs its work with warranties that run from five years up to a lifetime depending on the job, with same-day and emergency service available for the plumbing and drain problems that cannot wait for a scheduled appointment.

Trenchless Services

Video Camera Inspection

As with any trenchless job, the process starts with a camera run through the line to confirm the condition of the pipe and identify where the problem actually is before any repair method is chosen.

Trenchless Sewer Line Repair

For sewer lines that have failed to roots, cracking, or age, Plumber of Tucson offers trenchless repair as an alternative to full excavation, aimed at getting the line working again without tearing up desert landscaping, a driveway, or a patio.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business and customer record, and the equipment and warranty backing the crews bring to their work. Plumber of Tucson's two decades of licensed, insured service in the Tucson market cleared those checks.
