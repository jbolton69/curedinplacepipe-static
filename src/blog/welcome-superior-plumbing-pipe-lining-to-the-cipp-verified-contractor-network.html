Trenchless Services

Superior Plumbing & Pipe Lining offers the full sequence of work that a proper cured-in-place pipe project requires, for both residential and commercial properties.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Every lining job starts with a look inside the pipe. Superior runs video inspections to locate cracks, channeling, offset joints, root intrusion, and the scale buildup that is so common in Florida's older cast iron. The footage tells the customer exactly what they are dealing with and whether lining is the right fix.

Hydro Jetting and Descaling

A liner only bonds well to a clean host pipe. Superior's crews clean and descale the existing line with high-pressure jetting before any liner goes in, which also serves as a standalone service for drains that simply need their flow restored.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

Superior's core trenchless service is CIPP lining: a resin-saturated liner is positioned inside the damaged pipe and cured into a smooth, jointless new pipe within the old one. The approach is especially well suited to the cast iron drain lines that run beneath the slabs of thousands of Florida homes built from the 1950s through the 1970s, where digging means breaking up the floor of the house.

Spin Cast Lining

For larger-diameter lines, manholes, and structures where a traditional felt liner is not the best fit, Superior also offers spin cast (centrifugally cast) lining, applying a structural coating to the interior of the pipe.

Commercial Pipe Rehabilitation

Restaurants, condominium buildings, hotels, and healthcare facilities across the Gulf Coast rely on Superior to rehabilitate drain and sewer lines without closing for construction. Trenchless work lets a business keep operating while its underground plumbing is renewed.

Warranties That Back the Work

Superior stands behind its lining with a 10-year structural warranty on the installed liner and a 2-year warranty on craftsmanship and labor. Any contractor can promise a pipe will last; putting a written warranty on it is what separates a verified installer from the rest.
