Last updated: October 23, 2026

CuredInPlacePipe.net ("the site," "we," "us") is operated by Natural Media. The site helps property owners find prequalified cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) and trenchless sewer contractors, and it lets contractors apply to be listed. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, and the choices you have. It applies to curedinplacepipe.net and to the phone numbers published on it.

Information you give us

Most of the information we hold is information you type into one of our forms or tell us on the phone:

  • Estimate and contractor requests. Your name, phone number, email address, the city or address where the work is needed, and whatever you tell us about the sewer or drain problem.
  • Contractor listing applications. Company name, contact name, phone, email, business address, website, license number, years in business, Google rating and review count, crew and equipment details, and the service areas you cover.
  • Phone calls. When you call a number shown on this site, we receive your phone number, the number you dialed, the date and time, and the length of the call.

Call recording and transcription

The phone numbers on this site are call-tracking numbers. They forward to the contractor who serves your area, and they tell us which page the call came from. Calls to these numbers may be recorded and transcribed so that the contractor has an accurate record of your request and so that we can confirm leads were delivered. Where state law requires it, you will hear a notice at the start of the call. If you prefer not to be recorded, say so and the call will not be recorded, or reach the contractor through the written form on their profile instead.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we log technical details about visits: IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the page that referred you, and the date and time of each request. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful and how visitors move through the site, and a click-fraud filter that screens traffic from our paid advertising. The site is served through Cloudflare, which also sees request logs as part of delivering and protecting the site. None of these tools identify you to us by name.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar identifiers: analytics cookies that distinguish one visit from another, a cookie that remembers a form you started, and security cookies set by Cloudflare. We do not use cookies to build an advertising profile of you or to follow you to unrelated websites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, although a form you were filling in may need to be started again.

How we use your information

  • To pass your estimate request to the CIPP Verified contractor who serves your area and to confirm that they received it.
  • To review and verify a contractor's application, including checking a license number against the issuing authority and looking at public reviews.
  • To answer your questions and follow up on a request you made.
  • To measure which pages and advertisements bring in requests, so we can improve the site.
  • To detect and block spam, abuse and automated submissions.

Who we share it with

When you ask for an estimate, your request goes to the contractor matched to your location, and we keep a copy. That contractor uses it to contact you about the job and is responsible for how they handle it after that. We also rely on a few service providers to run the site: a form-delivery service that emails submissions to us and to the contractor, a telephony provider that routes, records and transcribes tracking-number calls, Google Analytics, Cloudflare, and the email and hosting providers that store our mail and files. Each of them processes your information only to provide that service to us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade lead lists. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to enforce our terms, or to protect the safety of a person or the integrity of the site.

Contractor listings are public

If your company is accepted into the CIPP Verified program, the business information you supplied for your profile — company name, logo, business phone, service areas, website, description and project write-ups — is published on this site. Personal details you provided only for verification, such as a license number or an owner's direct line, are not published unless you ask us to include them.

How long we keep it

Estimate requests, application forms and call records are kept for as long as they are useful for serving the request, tracking lead delivery and resolving any disputes about a job, and are then deleted or anonymized. Aggregate analytics that no longer identify a visitor may be kept indefinitely.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form and tell us what you would like done; we will confirm within a reasonable time.
  • You can decline call recording at the start of any call.
  • You can opt out of any follow-up email by replying and asking us to stop.
  • You can limit analytics collection with your browser's cookie controls or Google's browser opt-out tools.

Residents of California and other states with consumer privacy laws have the rights described in those laws, including the right to know, to delete and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. We honor those requests regardless of where you live.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, form submissions are encrypted in transit, and access to lead and application records is limited to the people who need it to serve the request. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without authorization, but we take reasonable steps to prevent it.

About the CIPP Verified listing — do your own diligence

Contractors listed on this site have gone through our prequalification: at the time of listing we confirmed that the company holds an active contractor's license, has at least five years in business, carries a strong public review history, and performs CIPP or other trenchless work with its own crews rather than subcontracting it. We do not verify insurance coverage or workers' compensation, we do not supervise or guarantee any contractor's work, and a listing can go out of date as licenses lapse, ownership changes or crews turn over.

The listing is a starting point, not a substitute for your own checks. Before hiring any contractor — from this site or anywhere else — confirm the license is current with your state licensing board, ask for a certificate of insurance naming you as certificate holder, get the scope, price and warranty in writing, and ask for recent references in your area. CuredInPlacePipe.net is not a party to any contract between you and a contractor, and we are not responsible for the quality, timeliness or outcome of their work. If you have a problem with a listed contractor, we want to know about it, and we will review the listing, but your legal remedies are with the contractor.

Children

This site is intended for property owners, managers and contractors. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has submitted a form, let us know and we will remove it.

Links to other sites

Contractor profiles link to each contractor's own website, and articles link to municipal, utility and industry sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
