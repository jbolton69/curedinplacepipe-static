Trenchless Services

Sewer Techs provides the full sequence of work a cured-in-place pipe project needs, for residential and commercial properties alike.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Every job starts inside the pipe. A video inspection locates the cracks, offset joints, bellies, and root intrusion that are typical of older Washington laterals, and it shows the customer whether lining is the right repair or whether a section needs to be replaced first.

Hydro Jetting and Root Removal

A liner bonds only to a clean host pipe. Sewer Techs clears roots, scale, and grease with high-pressure water jetting before any liner goes in. For drains that simply need their flow back, jetting is also offered on its own.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

The company's primary service is CIPP lining: a resin-saturated liner is positioned inside the existing pipe and cured into a smooth, jointless new pipe within the old one. It is the right fix for the cracked concrete and clay side sewers that run under Puget Sound lawns, and it means the mature cedars, firs, and landscaping that make these neighborhoods what they are stay exactly where they are.

Sectional Point Repair

When only a few feet of pipe have failed, a short structural liner can be set over that section alone, repairing the defect without lining the whole run or opening the ground above it.

Pipe Bursting

For lines that have collapsed or lost their grade, where a liner cannot restore the pipe, Sewer Techs replaces the lateral by pipe bursting: a new HDPE pipe is pulled through the path of the old one from two small access pits, with no open trench between them.
