Trenchless Services Available from CPC Trenchless

CPC Trenchless offers solutions for evaluating, preparing, and rehabilitating damaged pipe systems.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

CIPP lining creates a new, seamless pipe inside an existing damaged pipeline.

A resin-saturated liner is inserted into the original pipe and cured in place. Once hardened, the liner forms a durable interior surface that can seal cracks, bridge damaged joints, improve flow, and restore structural integrity.

CIPP can be used in many sewer, drain, and stormwater applications, depending on pipe condition, diameter, access, and project requirements.

Sewer and Drain Camera Inspections

A video inspection allows technicians to examine the inside of a pipeline without excavation.

CPC Trenchless can use camera inspection equipment to identify conditions such as:

Cracks and fractures

Root intrusion

Grease or debris buildup

Corrosion and scaling

Misaligned or separated joints

Partial collapses

Obstructions and restricted flow

The inspection helps determine whether cleaning, lining, a sectional repair, or another solution is appropriate.

Hydro Jetting and Pipe Cleaning

Proper cleaning is one of the most important steps in preparing a pipe for rehabilitation.

High-pressure hydro jetting can remove grease, sludge, roots, mineral deposits, and other materials that restrict flow or prevent a liner from bonding properly to the host pipe.

Cleaning may be provided as a standalone maintenance service or as part of the preparation process for a CIPP installation.

Sectional and Trenchless Point Repairs

Not every damaged pipeline requires full-length lining.

When deterioration is limited to a specific location, a sectional liner or trenchless point repair may be used to reinforce the affected area. This targeted approach can address isolated cracks, leaking joints, or localized damage without rehabilitating the entire pipe.

Commercial properties often contain pipelines beneath parking lots, concrete floors, landscaped areas, mechanical rooms, and other difficult-to-access locations.
