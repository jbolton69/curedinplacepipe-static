CuredInPlacePipe.net is pleased to welcome Lining & Coating Solutions, LLC of Olive Branch, Mississippi, as the newest member of our CIPP Verified Contractor Network, and as our verified installer for the state of Mississippi.

Like our other recent additions on the commercial and industrial side, Lining & Coating Solutions fills a specific need: manufacturing plants, power plants, chemical plants, and hospitals cannot take a pipe system offline for a routine excavation, and they need a contractor who treats rehabilitation as a specialty rather than a side offering. We are glad to add a contractor built exactly for that work.

About Lining & Coating Solutions

Lining & Coating Solutions, LLC is based in Olive Branch, Mississippi, just outside Memphis, with a second crew based in Tampa, Florida. General manager Steve Palmer built the company's lining and coating side of the business after years running a plumbing department, and that side of the business has since grown to become the company's primary line of work.

The client base is mostly manufacturing facilities, power plants, chemical plants, hospitals, and other industrial and commercial properties whose pipe systems cannot be shut down for an excavation. Crews descale and flush cast iron and other pipe before lining it, and use a spincast application method on smaller-diameter lines where it performs better than a blow-in epoxy.

Trenchless Services

Pipe Descaling and Flushing

Cast iron mainlines accumulate decades of scale and buildup. Lining & Coating Solutions cleans a line thoroughly before any coating or liner goes in, so the finished job bonds properly and lasts.

Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining

For mainline and lateral runs, the company installs cured-in-place liners that create a new, jointless pipe inside the old one, without opening a floor, a parking lot, or a street.

Spincast Pipe Coating

On smaller-diameter lines, the company applies a spincast coating rather than a blow-in epoxy where it gives a better, more consistent result.

Service Area

Lining & Coating Solutions is based in Olive Branch and covers the Memphis metro area spanning Mississippi, Tennessee, and Arkansas, with its Tampa crew covering projects in Florida.

Commercial and industrial property owners in the Memphis area can learn more about Lining & Coating Solutions' services, or on the Lining & Coating Solutions profile here on CuredInPlacePipe.net.

What CIPP Verified Means

The CIPP Verified Contractor designation is a prequalification, not a directory anyone can buy into. Before a contractor is added to this network we review licensing, time in business and customer record, and the equipment and specialization the crews bring to rehabilitation work. Lining & Coating Solutions' deep focus on commercial and industrial lining and coating cleared those checks.
